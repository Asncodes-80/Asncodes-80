# Talk slider artwork

Mode: built-in image_gen, three independent generations.

Original concept artwork for the three featured talks, rather than video
frames or photographs of the speaker. Each final asset is a 1672 × 941 WebP in
public/images/talks/backdrops/. Original generated PNGs are retained in the
image-generation library.

## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic 16:9 landscape background
for the featured slider of a personal software engineer's website. Create a
polished high-end film key-art background, a serious cyberpunk graphic novel
atmosphere rendered as tangible 3D concept art. Cohesive Dracula palette:
charcoal #21222c and #282a36, violet #bd93f9, restrained cyan #8be9fd and
green #50fa7b accent light. Dark but detailed, disciplined composition, large
calm shadows on both outer thirds for overlaid interface text, central focal
subject stays visible in a mobile center crop. Fine film grain, realistic
materials, atmospheric depth, restrained glow. No words, no letters, no code
text, no typography, no logos, no watermark, no interface, no portrait, no
clutter. This must be scene artwork, not a website mockup.

## ai-human.webp

Primary request: visualize human direction of AI coding tools. In a quiet
futuristic studio, a single natural human hand reaches toward a precise
suspended lattice of translucent computing modules. The fingertips almost
touch a small glowing node, with organized branching violet and cyan light
paths flowing through the structure. The hand and the luminous system feel
collaborative rather than adversarial. No face, no robot, no brain icon.
Cinematic close-up with dark negative space and tactile detail.

## software-architecture.webp

Primary request: visualize the resilience and architecture of large software
systems. A monumental organized city of modular server-like towers rises from
a dark platform, connected by a few elegant luminous bridges. One disconnected
module is dim while alternate green light paths continue through the remaining
structures. View from a low wide angle, cyberpunk architectural scale, violet
rim lighting and restrained cyan volumetric haze. Geometric, coherent, calm
and technically evocative rather than a busy real city.

## mcp-creative.webp

Primary request: visualize AI connecting to 3D creative tools through MCP. In
a dark precision design studio, a beautifully crafted sculptural assembly of a
chrome torus, a faceted translucent cube and a small floating sphere is partly
rendered as a fine luminous wireframe and partly solid. A few elegant light
connections link the forms, suggesting creative tools working together. Violet
and cyan rim light with one restrained warm orange accent, glossy and frosted
material contrast. A sophisticated cinematic product render, central
composition with generous quiet surrounding space.

The full prompt for each asset is the shared prompt followed by its brief.
Interface text, gradients, and transitions are rendered by the website.
